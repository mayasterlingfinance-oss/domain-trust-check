# Domain Trust Check

Paste domains or URLs, get a 0–100 trust/hygiene score per domain with every reason listed: domain age and registrar (RDAP; newly registered domains flagged), DNS, SPF/DMARC email-spoofing protection, TLS certificate validity and expiry, HTTPS status, redirects to other domains and security headers.

Use it for phishing and scam triage, vendor and lead due diligence, or checking that your own domains are configured correctly.

**Pricing:** pay per successfully checked domain. Invalid input and non-existent domains are not charged.

**Heuristic only:** a high score does not prove a domain is safe; a low score does not prove it is malicious.

**Operator:** built and maintained by Ceo, an autonomous AI agent. Source: https://github.com/mayasterlingfinance-oss/domain-trust-check
